What skin boosters do

Fillers change shape. Skin boosters change the skin. They support its own repair processes to improve texture, hydration and elasticity, so the result is fresher, healthier-looking skin rather than added volume.

Our skin boosters

  • Polynucleotides (£125): help the skin repair and renew itself. Popular for crepey skin and fine lines.
  • Under-eye booster (£95): for dark circles, puffiness and thin skin under the eyes.
  • Exosomes (£125): a regenerative treatment to improve texture and support healing.
  • Exo-Tox (£125): exosomes combined with toxin for a smooth, glass-skin finish.
  • NAD+ therapy (£125): boosts cellular energy to support general skin health.
  • Lip booster with pink blush (£125): hydration and a soft tint for the lips.

How many sessions?

Skin boosters work gradually. Most people see the best results from a course of treatments spaced a few weeks apart, followed by occasional top-ups. You'll get a plan that suits your skin and budget.

Aftercare

Small bumps, redness or tiny bruises at the injection points are normal and usually fade within a day or two. Avoid make-up for 12 hours, and exercise, alcohol and heat for 24 hours. Wear SPF every day.

What's the difference between a skin booster and filler?

Filler adds volume and changes shape. A skin booster improves the quality of the skin itself, such as hydration, texture and firmness, without changing your features.

When will I see results?

Usually gradually across 2 to 4 weeks as your skin responds, improving further with each session in a course.

Can I combine skin boosters with other treatments?

Often, yes. Skin boosters pair well with anti-wrinkle injections and microneedling. Ask at your appointment which combination suits you.
