Bridal hair & makeup

Your bridal look is planned around you: your dress, your style and the feel of the day. The three-hour appointment covers full makeup and hairstyling, so nothing feels rushed on the morning.

Hair and makeup for events

  • Glam makeup (£59, 1 hour): full-face makeup for parties, nights out and photoshoots.
  • Hair & makeup (£98, 2 hours): professional styling and makeup together.

Getting your skin wedding-ready

If you'd like your skin at its best on the day, start early. Skin boosters, microneedling and anti-wrinkle injections take time to settle, so book them several weeks to a few months ahead. Dermaplaning a few days before is a good final step.

Booking your date

Weekend dates in spring and summer fill up quickly, so it's worth booking as soon as your date is set. We're open on Saturdays and Sundays.

When should I book bridal hair and makeup?

As soon as your wedding date is set, especially for a weekend in spring or summer.

Can I have aesthetic treatments before my wedding?

Yes, with enough time for results to settle. Plan injectable treatments at least 2 to 4 weeks before the day, and skin courses a few months ahead.
