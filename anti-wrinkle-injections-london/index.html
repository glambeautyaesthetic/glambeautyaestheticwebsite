How anti-wrinkle injections work

Many lines on the upper face come from repeated muscle movement: raising your eyebrows, frowning, smiling. Anti-wrinkle injections relax the specific muscles behind those lines, so the skin above them smooths out.

Anti-wrinkle injections are a prescription-only medicine, so your suitability is assessed before any treatment.

Areas we treat

  • Forehead lines, frown lines and crow's feet: 1 area £90, 2 areas £114, 3 areas £150.
  • Brow lift, lip flip, gummy smile and downturned mouth corners (DAO): £98.
  • Jaw slimming (masseter) or nose slimming: £124.
  • Neck anti-wrinkle: £163.
  • Traptox, for a slimmer shoulder line: £228.
  • Excessive underarm sweating (hyperhidrosis): £228.

What to expect

Treatment takes 20 to 45 minutes depending on the number of areas. The injections are very fine and quick, and most people find them easy to tolerate. Results start to show after 3 to 5 days and reach full effect at around two weeks.

Aftercare

Stay upright for 4 hours after treatment and avoid rubbing or massaging the area. Skip strenuous exercise, saunas and alcohol for 24 hours.

How long do anti-wrinkle injections last?

Usually around 3 to 4 months, after which muscle movement gradually returns.

Will I still be able to move my face?

Yes. The aim is to soften lines, not freeze your expression. The dose is adjusted to how much movement you want to keep.

How many areas should I have treated?

Most people start with forehead and frown lines (2 areas, £114) or add crow's feet (3 areas, £150). You'll get a recommendation for your face at your appointment.

Is there an age limit?

Yes. By UK law, anti-wrinkle injections for cosmetic reasons are only available to people aged 18 or older.
