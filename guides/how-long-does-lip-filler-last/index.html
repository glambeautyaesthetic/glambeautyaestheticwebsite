How long does lip filler last?

Usually around 6 to 12 months. Here's what affects it, how to tell it's fading and when to top up.

By Glam Beauty Aesthetics · 12 November 2026 · 4 min read

Key points

  • Lip filler usually lasts around 6 to 12 months.
  • How long it lasts depends on the product, the amount and your own metabolism.
  • Your first treatment may fade a little sooner than later top-ups.
  • Hyaluronic acid filler can be dissolved if you ever change your mind.

The short answer

Most people find their lip filler lasts around 6 to 12 months. Lip filler is a hyaluronic acid gel, a substance found naturally in your skin, and your body gradually breaks it down over time. That's why results fade softly rather than disappearing all at once.

What affects how long it lasts

  • The product: some fillers are softer and more flexible, others are firmer. Your practitioner chooses one to suit the look you want.
  • The amount: a subtle 0.5 ml treatment may fade a little sooner than a fuller 1 ml treatment.
  • Your metabolism: everyone breaks down hyaluronic acid at a slightly different rate.
  • Movement: lips move all day as you talk, eat and smile, so lip filler tends to fade faster than filler in less mobile areas such as the chin or cheeks.
  • Lifestyle: very frequent intense exercise and smoking are often linked with results fading sooner.

Your first treatment

Many clients find their first lip filler fades a little sooner than later treatments. Starting with a smaller amount and building gradually is still the most reliable way to keep your lips in proportion with your face.

Signs your filler is fading

You may notice your lips looking slightly less defined at the border, a little less full, or less hydrated. There's no fixed timetable, so it's simply a case of booking a top-up when you'd like to refresh the look.

Topping up

A top-up is usually a smaller amount than your first treatment. At Glam Beauty Aesthetics, lip filler starts from £90 for 0.5 ml, and a 1 ml treatment is £100. If you'd prefer hydration and a soft tint rather than added volume, the lip booster with pink blush is £125.

Can lip filler be dissolved?

Yes. Hyaluronic acid filler can be dissolved with an enzyme called hyaluronidase. It's £125 per 1 ml, and it's always discussed with you first.
