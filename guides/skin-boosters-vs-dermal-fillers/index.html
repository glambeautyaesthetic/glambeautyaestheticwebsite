Skin boosters vs dermal fillers.

Fillers add shape and volume. Skin boosters improve the quality of your skin. Here's how to tell which suits you.

By Glam Beauty Aesthetics · 12 November 2026 · 4 min read

Key points

  • Dermal fillers add volume and shape to specific areas.
  • Skin boosters improve hydration, texture, glow and fine lines across the skin.
  • Skin boosters are usually a course. Fillers are usually a single treatment.
  • Many people combine both for a balanced, natural result.

The main difference

Dermal fillers add volume and shape to a specific area, such as the lips, cheeks, jawline or chin. Skin boosters don't change your shape. Instead they improve the quality of the skin itself: hydration, texture, firmness and glow.

Dermal fillers

  • Best for: fuller lips, defined cheekbones, a sharper jawline, a balanced chin or softer lines around the mouth.
  • How they work: a hyaluronic acid gel is placed precisely to add structure.
  • Results: visible straight away, settling at around two weeks.
  • Price: dermal filler is £100 per 1 ml, and lip filler starts from £90 for 0.5 ml.

Skin boosters

  • Best for: dull or dehydrated skin, fine lines, crepey texture, dark circles and an overall lack of glow.
  • How they work: they are spread across the skin to hydrate it and support your own collagen.
  • Results: build gradually, usually over a course of treatments.
  • Options: polynucleotides (£125), exosomes (£125) and Radiesse (£325), which supports collagen for firmer skin.

Which one suits you?

If you'd like to change a shape, such as fuller lips or a more defined jaw, a filler is usually the answer. If your main concern is how your skin looks and feels, a skin booster is the better place to start. Many clients combine both: a skin booster for healthy, glowing skin and a small amount of filler for definition.

The easiest way to decide is a free consultation, where we look at your skin and features and suggest a plan that suits you.

Questions

What is the difference between skin boosters and fillers?

Dermal fillers add volume and shape to specific areas. Skin boosters improve the quality of the skin itself, such as hydration, texture and glow.

Can I have skin boosters and filler together?

Yes. Many clients combine them for a balanced result. Your practitioner will plan the timing with you at your consultation.

How many skin booster sessions will I have?

Skin boosters usually work best as a course. Your practitioner will recommend the right number at your free consultation.

This guide is general information, not medical advice. Everyone is different, so your practitioner will talk you through what suits you at your free consultation.
