When do anti-wrinkle injections work?

Results start after 3 to 5 days and reach full effect at around two weeks. Here's the full timeline.

By Glam Beauty Aesthetics · 12 November 2026 · 3 min read

Key points

  • You'll usually see the first changes after 3 to 5 days.
  • The full effect shows at around two weeks.
  • Results usually last around 3 to 4 months.
  • Stay upright for 4 hours and avoid rubbing the area after treatment.

The timeline

  • Straight after: you may see small bumps where the injections went in. These usually settle within about 20 minutes.
  • Day 3 to 5: the treated muscles start to relax and lines begin to soften.
  • Around 2 weeks: you see the full effect. This is the right time to review your result.
  • 3 to 4 months: movement gradually returns and lines slowly reappear, which is when most people book again.

How they work

Many lines on the upper face come from repeated movement, such as raising your eyebrows or frowning. Anti-wrinkle injections relax the specific muscles behind those lines, so the skin above them smooths out while you keep a natural expression.

Anti-wrinkle injections are a prescription-only medicine, so your suitability is always assessed at a consultation before any treatment.

Aftercare

  • Stay upright for 4 hours after treatment.
  • Avoid rubbing or massaging the treated area.
  • Skip strenuous exercise, saunas and alcohol for 24 hours.
