Lip filler swelling and aftercare.

What to expect in the days after lip filler, when the final result shows, and how to look after your lips.

By Glam Beauty Aesthetics · 12 November 2026 · 4 min read

Key points

  • Swelling is usually most noticeable for the first 1 to 2 days.
  • Most swelling settles within about a week.
  • Your final result shows at around two weeks.
  • Avoid lipstick, alcohol, strenuous exercise and heat for the first 24 hours.

Day by day

  • Day 1 to 2: swelling is usually at its most noticeable. Lips can look bigger than the final result and may feel firm or slightly uneven. This is normal.
  • Day 3 to 4: swelling starts to settle and your lips begin to look softer and more even.
  • Day 5 to 7: most swelling has gone. Any small bruises are usually fading too.
  • Around 2 weeks: the filler has settled and you see your final result. This is the best time to judge the shape.

Everyone heals a little differently, so don't worry if your timeline is slightly longer or shorter.

Bruising

Small bruises can appear where the filler was placed. They usually fade within a few days to a week. If you have an event coming up, book your treatment at least two weeks before.

Aftercare for the first 24 hours

  • Avoid lipstick and lip products.
  • Avoid alcohol and strenuous exercise.
  • Avoid heat such as saunas, steam rooms and hot yoga.
  • Try not to press, massage or kiss firmly on the lips.
  • A cold compress wrapped in a clean cloth can help with swelling.

You'll get aftercare advice before you leave, and you can contact us any time with questions.

When to contact us straight away

Contact the clinic straight away, or seek urgent medical help, if you notice pain that is getting worse rather than better, skin that turns pale, white, blue or dusky, or any changes to your vision. These are rare, but they need to be checked quickly.

Questions

How long does lip filler swelling last?

Swelling is usually most noticeable for the first 1 to 2 days and mostly settles within about a week. The final result shows at around two weeks.

Is it normal for lips to look lumpy after filler?

Some firmness or unevenness in the first few days is common while swelling settles. If you're worried, contact the clinic and we'll check.

When can I wear lipstick after lip filler?

Wait at least 24 hours before using lipstick or other lip products.

This guide is general information, not medical advice. Everyone is different, so your practitioner will talk you through what suits you at your free consultation.
