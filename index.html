    Is the consultation really free?

    Yes. Your consultation is free and there's no obligation to book a treatment. You'll talk through what you'd like to change and your medical history, and leave with a clear plan and price.

    Is the practitioner qualified?

    Yes. Your treatment is carried out by our Level 4 advanced qualified professional, who has more than 20 years' experience in the beauty industry and talks everything through with you, checking your medical history before starting.

    How do I book?

    Tap any "Book" button, choose your treatment and a time, and confirm. Booking is handled securely through our booking page on Glossa, and you can book at any time of day.

    Are you open at weekends?

    Yes. We are open seven days a week: Monday to Friday until 5:30pm, and Saturday and Sunday from 9:30am to 4pm.

    Will my results look natural?

    That is the aim of every treatment here. The amount of product and where it goes are planned around your features and what you want to change, so you look refreshed rather than "done".

    How long do anti-wrinkle injections and fillers last?

    It varies from person to person. As a guide, anti-wrinkle results usually last around 3 to 4 months, and dermal fillers around 6 to 12 months depending on the area. You'll get a realistic timeframe for your treatment at your appointment.

    Is there an age limit?

    Yes. By UK law, anti-wrinkle injections and dermal fillers for cosmetic reasons are only available to people aged 18 or older.

    Can filler be dissolved if I don't like it?

    Yes. Hyaluronidase dissolves hyaluronic acid filler that has moved or that you no longer want. It costs £125 per 1 ml.

    How much do treatments cost?

    Treatments start at £20 for a vitamin B12 injection. Lip filler starts at £90, anti-wrinkle at £90 for one area, and skin boosters from £95. Every price is listed in the treatment menu above.
