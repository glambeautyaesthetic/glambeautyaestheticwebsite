What lip filler does

Lip filler is a hyaluronic acid gel, a substance found naturally in your skin. It can add volume, sharpen the border of the lips, correct small asymmetries and hydrate lips that have thinned with age.

The amount and placement are planned around your face. For a first treatment, 0.5 ml is often enough for a subtle change. 1 ml gives more noticeable shape and volume.

Which option suits you?

  • Lip filler, 0.5 ml (£90): a subtle first treatment or a top-up.
  • Lip filler, 1 ml (£100): more shape and volume.
  • Lip booster with pink blush (£125): hydration and a soft natural tint rather than added volume.
  • Lip flip (£98): a small anti-wrinkle treatment that turns the top lip slightly outwards, with no filler.

Your appointment

You start by talking through the look you want and your medical history. Most lip fillers contain a local anaesthetic to keep you comfortable, and the treatment takes around 30 minutes. You'll see a difference straight away. Swelling means the final result settles within about two weeks.

Aftercare

Some swelling, tenderness and possibly bruising are normal for a few days. For the first 24 hours, avoid lipstick, alcohol, strenuous exercise and heat such as saunas. You'll get aftercare advice before you leave.

How long does lip filler last?

Usually around 6 to 12 months. It varies with the product used, your metabolism and your lifestyle.

Will my lips look natural?

That is the aim. Starting with a smaller amount and building gradually is the most reliable way to keep lips in proportion with your face.

Does lip filler hurt?

Most people find it uncomfortable rather than painful. Most fillers contain a local anaesthetic that numbs the area as the treatment goes on.

Can lip filler be dissolved?

Yes. Hyaluronic acid filler can be dissolved with hyaluronidase, which costs £125 per 1 ml.
