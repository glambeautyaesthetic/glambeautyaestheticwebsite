Multi-vitamin microneedling

Microneedling (£120) creates tiny channels in the skin using a pen with ultra-fine points. This prompts the skin to repair itself and produce fresh collagen, and helps a vitamin serum absorb more deeply. It helps with dull skin, uneven texture, open pores and mild scarring.

Hair growth therapy

For thinning hair, scalp injections are combined with microneedling (£125) to stimulate the hair follicles. It is usually done as a course, and results build gradually across several months.

Dermaplaning

Dermaplaning (£59) is a gentle exfoliation with a sterile blade that removes dead skin and fine facial hair. Skin looks brighter straight away, make-up goes on more smoothly, and there's no downtime.

Korean glass facial

The Korean glass facial (£50) is a multi-step hydrating facial inspired by Korean skincare. It cleanses, gently exfoliates and deeply hydrates the skin for a smooth, dewy, light-reflecting “glass skin” finish. There's no downtime, so it's a lovely treat before an event.

Cosmelan peel

Cosmelan (£553, aftercare products included) is a professional depigmentation peel for stubborn melasma, sun spots and uneven tone. The mask is applied in clinic and removed at home several hours later, followed by a home-care routine. Expect redness and peeling for several days. Results develop during the following weeks.

Aftercare

After microneedling, expect redness similar to mild sunburn for a day or two. Avoid make-up for 24 hours, and exercise, swimming and heat for 48 hours. Wear SPF 50 daily after any of these treatments.

How many microneedling sessions are recommended?

Usually a course of 3 to 6 sessions, about four weeks apart, depending on your skin and goals.

Can I have dermaplaning before an event?
